# Art And Sound

- Tiny Town and Tiny Dungeon tile packs: Kenney, CC0. https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town and https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-dungeon
- RPG Audio: Kenney, CC0. https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
- House cover: original AI-generated illustration for this project.
- Hero sprites: original procedural pixel art, generated by scripts/build-assets.ts.
- Live missing sprites: generated as bounded pixel grids by local Claude Code, or raster images by the optional OpenAI provider.

Reference screenshots in the workspace are not distributed in the application.
